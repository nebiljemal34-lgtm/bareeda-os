# HeyGen Master Prompt: Bareeda Injera — Customer How-To Video

> **How to use:** Copy the prompt below into HeyGen Video Agent. Attach the screenshots listed in the "Assets to Prepare" section before generating.

---

## Video Settings

- **Avatar:** Professional female or male presenter, warm and friendly, business-casual attire. Avatar appears in a small circular or corner overlay during intro and outro only. During walkthrough scenes, the avatar is hidden and the screen recording takes full width.
- **Voice:** Clear, warm, conversational English. Moderate pace. Slight enthusiasm when describing products. Pause briefly after each click so viewers can follow.
- **Duration target:** 4–5 minutes total.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (landscape) for YouTube and website embedding.
- **Style:** Clean, modern, minimal. Navy blue (#0a1628) and gold (#c6b79b) accent colors matching the Bareeda brand. Rounded corners on all UI callouts.
- **Captions:** On, bottom-center, white text with subtle dark background.

## Production Notes for HeyGen

1. **Mouse cursor style:** Use a standard white arrow cursor with a subtle gold ring highlight on hover. Clicks should show a brief gold ripple or pulse animation.
2. **Transitions:** Use smooth crossfade between scenes (0.3s). No hard cuts.
3. **Pacing:** Allow 1–2 seconds of silence after each click so viewers can absorb what happened.
4. **Zoom:** When highlighting specific UI elements (price, button, form field), use a gentle 1.2x zoom into that area, then zoom back out.
5. **Brand consistency:** All highlight boxes, borders, and callout colors should use gold (#c6b79b). Backgrounds are navy (#0a1628).
6. **No emoji:** Use clean icons only — no emoji in any on-screen text.
7. **Language:** English narration with Amharic text visible on the actual website screenshots (do not translate the Amharic in the UI).
